# The Student Who Let AI Do Everything

Theme: AI Should assist human work, not replace human responsibility.

## Story concept

A Computer Science student discovers AI and initially uses it to understand difficult programming concepts. His productivity improves, and he becomes excited about what he can accomplish.

Gradually, he begins asking AI to complete everything: assignments, projects, presentations, and even decisions he should make himself. His work looks impressive, but he no longer understands what he submits. When a real problem arises, he cannot solve it independently.

He eventually realizes that AI should help him develop his abilities, not eliminate his responsibility to learn and work.

## Connection to the cultural mandate

Human beings are called to work and cultivate creation. AI can help us fulfill that responsibility, but using it to avoid responsibility undermines the purpose of work.

## Practical takeaway

Use AI to learn, explore, and improve your work. Do not let it replace your own thinking, integrity, and responsibility.

## Why this works

It is relatable to university students and relatively easy to produce with AI-generated animation. It also creates a clear narrative with a beginning, conflict, and resolution.
